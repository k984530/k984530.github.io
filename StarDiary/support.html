Download & One-Time Purchase

Star Diary - Stella Notes is available as a single one-time paid app on the App Store and Google Play, with all features included and no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads. Store purchases and refunds are handled directly by Apple App Store or Google Play under each store's one-time app purchase refund policy.

v1.0 → 2.0 Migration & JSON Backup

Existing Star Diary v1.0 users automatically migrate their local diary entries when updating to 2.0, with all features included at no extra charge. Because entries are stored 100% locally on your device, use the in-app JSON export backup feature regularly before switching devices or reinstalling.
