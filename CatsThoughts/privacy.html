Privacy Policy (English)

Cat's Thoughts (고양이의 생각) 2.0 · iOS: id6743672789 · Android: com.aly.CatsThoughts · Effective: 2026-11-25

1. Overview

Cat's Thoughts (고양이의 생각) 2.0 is a 100% free 3D cat tower building game across 5 growth stages designed to run offline with complete privacy. This Privacy Policy explains that the App collects no personal data, stores game progress 100% locally on your device, and explains how optional support emails are handled.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection and No Accounts

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data from inside the App. Cat's Thoughts 2.0 requires no registration and no login, and does not access or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, photos, or device identifiers.

3. 100% Local On-Device Storage

All game progress data—including your tower placement, in-game coins, and unlock states—is stored 100% locally inside your device's app local storage (localStorage). None of this data leaves your device or is uploaded to any developer or third-party server.

4. No External Servers, No Ads, No AD_ID, No Tracking or Analytics SDKs

  • No External Server Transmission or Third-Party Sharing: The App does not connect to external game servers and never collects, transmits, shares, rents, or sells user data to third parties.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID) or Tracking: The App does not request or read any Advertising ID (AD_ID), performs no cross-app tracking, and includes no advertising SDKs.
  • No External Analytics SDKs: The App contains no third-party analytics, telemetry, or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • 100% Free — No Billing SDKs: Because there are no paid in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads, the App includes no payment or ad mediation SDKs.

5. Data Retention, Deletion, and Unrecoverable Local Data

Because your tower placement, coins, and unlock states reside exclusively in your device's local storage, you have full control over them directly on your device. Deleting the App, clearing app storage, or resetting your device permanently deletes your local game data, and the developer cannot recover it.

6. Optional Support Emails

If you voluntarily contact us by email at hello@choiwon.dev, we receive your email address, your message, and any device or app version details you choose to include. We use that information solely to reply to your inquiry and resolve technical issues, never share or sell it to third parties, and delete or retain it only as long as needed to complete your support request.

7. Children's Privacy

Cat's Thoughts 2.0 does not collect personal information from anyone—including children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction)—and contains no ads, tracking, accounts, or paid purchases. If a parent or legal guardian believes a child has voluntarily sent personal information through a support email, please contact hello@choiwon.dev and we will promptly delete the correspondence.

8. App Distribution Platforms

  • Apple App Store — free app distribution on iOS (App ID: id6743672789).
  • Google Play — free app distribution on Android (Package: com.aly.CatsThoughts).
  • Email service providers — delivering support messages you voluntarily send to hello@choiwon.dev.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App's features or applicable legal requirements change, we will update this Privacy Policy and post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Related Policies and Contact

See our Terms of Service for free-to-play and local storage terms, or visit Support for help and FAQ.

Privacy inquiries: hello@choiwon.dev
